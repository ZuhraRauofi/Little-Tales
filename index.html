About Us

Little Tales is a cozy online library of children's stories. We create gentle, colorful tales for children and the parents who read to them.

Reading stories is one of the best gifts for a growing mind. It builds vocabulary and imagination, teaches kindness and courage, and gives families a calm moment together before bed.

  • 📖 Builds language and listening skills
  • 💛 Teaches kindness and empathy
  • 🌙 Makes bedtime calm and happy
